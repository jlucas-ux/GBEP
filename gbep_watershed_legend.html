GBEP subwatersheds are lower Galveston Bay subwatershed boundaries agreed on by GBEP and its subcommittee stakeholders. Each watershed represents a distinct drainage catchment flowing into Galveston Bay. Each project may impact multiple watersheds, therefore, the summed projects per watershed may be greater than the total number of projects.
